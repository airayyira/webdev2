5 FACTS ABOUT INUMAKI TOGE

Inumaki's Cursed Speech

Inumaki is a descendant of the Inumaki Clan and has inherited its Cursed Speech. He uses Cursed Speech to infuse his spoken words with cursed energy and force anyone who hears them to obey his commands.

Inumaki's first name's meaning may be a pun on his Cursed Speech.

Inumaki's surname contains the kanji for "dog" (狗 inu?) and "to roll up" (巻 maki?), and his first name contains "thorn/biting words" (棘 toge?).

His hobby is watching YouTube videos.

He likes to watch mukbang (Korean eating) videos on Youtube.
